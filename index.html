Marathon, Florida Keys Fishing Guide

Real fish photographs with quick-reference bait, Marathon-area habitat and harvest regulations. Built for recognizing your targets on the water.

Rating scale: ★★★★★ = exceptional. Eating rates table quality, Fun / Fight rates the experience on the rod, and Difficulty rates how challenging the species is to locate, hook and successfully land. Difficulty can vary a lot with season, guide quality and conditions.
Regulation warning: The Florida Keys cross state/federal and Atlantic/Gulf management boundaries, and seasons/quotas can change. These are planning summaries, not a substitute for the current FWC and NOAA rules on the day you fish.

🎣 Bait & lures

Live crabs, mullet, pinfish, threadfin herring; swimbaits and soft plastics.

📍 Where to catch

Seven Mile Bridge, channels and passes, flats, nearshore Gulf/Atlantic edges; moving tide.

⚖️ Regulations

Catch-and-release only. Hook-and-line only. Fish over 40 in. generally must remain in the water except limited record-tag circumstances. No snagging.

Deep reefs, wrecks and high-relief structure.
